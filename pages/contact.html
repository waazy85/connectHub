Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common ConnectHub enquiries.

Yes. Visit our Properties page to explore available property listings.

Yes. Landlords and agents can use the contact form to enquire about property listings and opportunities to work with the platform.

Can I enquire about land or commercial property?

Yes. Select the relevant enquiry type in the contact form and provide your preferred location and requirements.

How do I send my enquiry?

Complete the form and select Send Message. Your default email application will open with the enquiry prepared for you to review and send.
